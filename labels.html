QR labels

Stick an oven or furnace label next to each door. Sample labels hold just the sample code, which is what the app reads.

One code (T45), a range (T45-T60), or several separated by commas.

Dish stickers go on the side of each petri dish (polyester labels hold up at 105 °C). Each template is one page: the tray sits on it and each crucible goes on its numbered ring, with that spot's QR next to it. Load the furnace in the same order as the rings.
