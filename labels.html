Job cards and labels

Job cards (Moisture, Ash and VM)

One Moisture card (12), one Ash card (10) and one VM card (18) per balance per batch: print all three before the batch starts. The Moisture card is paper only (no QR): the worker writes the dish number used next to each code. The codes come from the Google Sheet allotment (Machine column). The worker scans the card's QR on the weighing phone, weighs W1 and W2 spot by spot, and scans it again for W3 when the crucibles are back.
