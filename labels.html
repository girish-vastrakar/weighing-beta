Job cards and labels

Job cards (Moisture, Ash and VM)

One Moisture card (12), one Ash card (10) and one VM card (18) per balance per batch: print all three before the batch starts. The Moisture card is paper only (no QR): the worker writes the dish number used next to each code. The codes come from the Google Sheet allotment (Machine column). The worker scans the card's QR on the weighing phone, weighs W1 and W2 spot by spot, and scans it again for W3 when the crucibles are back.

Reprint a saved card

The cards already saved for this weighing machine, by day: batch numbers start again at 1 every day. Batches not finished yet (W3 still to do) carry over to today. Reprint prints the same card again: nothing changes on the phones.

Reusable sample tags

Laminated cards that go on the reusable sample bags instead of stickers. Each QR holds only the tag number (TAG 07). The supervisor puts a tag on a sample in the weighing app and takes it off after MC, Ash and VM all have W3. Old sample stickers keep working. Cards are 50 × 70 mm, 16 on an A4 page: print at 100% (no fit to page), cut on the dashed lines, laminate, then punch the circle. To replace a lost tag, print just its number.

Supervisor: weighing app → Supervisor on → Tags on the home screen. Put the tags on the bags before printing the job cards, so the cards show each tag number.

Sample labels hold just the sample code, which is what the app reads.

One code (T45), a range (T45-T60), or several separated by commas.

Dish stickers go on the side of each petri dish (polyester labels hold up at 105 °C).
