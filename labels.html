Boards and labels

Stick an oven or furnace label next to each door. Sample labels hold just the sample code, which is what the app reads.

One code (T45), a range (T45-T60), or several separated by commas.

Dish stickers go on the side of each petri dish (polyester labels hold up at 105 °C). Each board is one A4 page: laminate it and keep it next to its balance (two Ash and two VM boards per balance). The app shows which ring to use; the worker puts the crucible on that number. Load the furnace in ring order, down each column.
